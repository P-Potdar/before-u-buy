Topic: Cost per use
- Cost per use is the purchase price divided by the expected number of uses over the relevant period.
- Frequency and expected lifespan should be based on actual planned use, not on a hoped-for pattern.
- Recurring costs are separate from the upfront purchase price and should be reviewed alongside it.
- A higher cost per use does not automatically mean a purchase is bad; it simply makes the value-to-use tradeoff more visible.
