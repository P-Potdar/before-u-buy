Topic: Sales and FOMO pressure
- Limited-time offers can create urgency, but urgency alone does not prove a purchase is necessary.
- FOMO often reflects social pressure, trend effects, or fear of missing out rather than a clear need.
- Waiting briefly can help separate genuine value from a short-lived emotional push.
- A lower price is only helpful when the item still fits a budget and a real plan for use.
