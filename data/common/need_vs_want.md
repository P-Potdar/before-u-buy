Topic: Need vs. want
- A need usually supports essential living costs, while a want is optional and often discretionary.
- Useful purchases often solve a problem or provide a clear benefit rather than matching a passing trend.
- Comparing a purchase against essentials, recurring costs, and savings helps keep spending aligned with goals.
- A short pause can help separate genuine need from social pressure, novelty, or habit.
