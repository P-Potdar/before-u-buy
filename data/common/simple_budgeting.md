Topic: Simple budgeting
- A simple budget compares planned spending against essentials, savings, and known recurring costs.
- A purchase is easier to evaluate when the user separates one-time cost from regular commitments.
- Savings and surplus are useful context, but they should be treated as user-provided information rather than assumed facts.
- Budgeting supports better decisions without assuming a person has more financial capacity than they stated.
