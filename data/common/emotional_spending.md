Topic: Emotional spending
- Emotional spending can be influenced by stress, reward-seeking, or a desire for relief after a difficult day.
- The decision is clearer when the user names the trigger, the purpose, and the alternatives.
- Tracking the pattern of spending can help separate a genuine need from a short-term mood-driven impulse.
- A pause, a comparison, and a budget check are decision-support tools rather than moral judgments.
