Topic: 30-day rule
- The 30-day rule is an optional reflection technique that can reduce impulsive decisions.
- Waiting for a month or another chosen period can reveal whether the urge comes from a real need or a temporary emotional trigger.
- It is not a scientifically guaranteed method for predicting regret or making a perfect purchase decision.
- If the item still makes sense after the waiting period, compare it to alternatives, total cost, and actual planned use.
