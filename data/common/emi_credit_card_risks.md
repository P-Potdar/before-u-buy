Topic: EMI and credit-card risks
- EMI plans can make a purchase feel smaller each month, but they still add cost and commitment.
- Credit-card interest can make a purchase more expensive than the sticker price if the balance is carried.
- A purchase should be reviewed using total cost, repayment plan, and whether the spend fits the existing budget.
- Paying over time can be reasonable when the plan is intentional and still affordable without penalty.
